JAN Beat Store — catalog update

This package preserves the existing React/Vite app, including the bilingual catalog, filters, preview player, cart, and the NOS MATAMOS beat data and local asset paths.

To apply it, use this folder as your project or copy its src/style.css into your project at src/style.css. The original project folder is not modified.

Visual changes: two larger beat cards per row on standard desktop/tablet widths, three at very wide widths, one on phones; consistent square cover art; subtle lime border, lift, shadow, and cover zoom on hover; visible focus styling; reduced-motion support.
